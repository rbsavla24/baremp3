      What gets removed — and what stays?

      Supported ID3v1/v2, APEv1/v2 and Lyrics3 tags, artwork, and Xing/Info/VBRI information frames at the start of audio sections are removed. Each output is checked for complete audio frames; archive entries are checked for size and checksum integrity. Removing that information frame can affect gapless playback, seeking or duration estimates.

      Audio content, filenames, spoken information, watermarks and identifiers hidden within audio frames remain. This does not guarantee anonymity or removal of every possible hidden identifier.

      Browser uploads do not carry your original macOS download-source attributes. Your browser or macOS may add new download attributes to the ZIP or extracted files; this page cannot clear filesystem attributes.

      New ZIP entries use a fixed date and contain no comments, extra fields or macOS sidecar files. Duplicate output paths stop the batch rather than overwrite a file. Unsupported or structurally invalid MP3s are excluded, with a reason shown in the results.

      Batch limits: 200 MP3s, 250 MB input, 500 MB expanded audio. ZIPs inside ZIPs are not processed. Encrypted, multi-volume and ZIP64 archives are unsupported.
